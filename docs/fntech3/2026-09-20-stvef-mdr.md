# Elite Force skeletal models and lighting

**Live-evidence correction (2026-09-21):** These probes used `r_renderer`,
which does not select fnTech3's backend. All fnTech3 live runs in this record
used GLx; Vulkan/RTX labels in the historical manifest identify the requested
test cases only. Source, native-vector and compiled-fixture evidence is
unchanged. See the [renderer audit](2026-09-21-stvef-renderer-verification.md).

This slice validates Raven's RDM5 version-2 models before renderer allocation,
restores Elite Force's LOD and held-frame rules, and corrects its ambient and
diffuse colour rounding in GLx, Vulkan and RTX. Both profiles remain behind
`-Dstvef-development=true`. Numerical parity and the windowed observations
below do not establish pixel-identical rendering on every renderer or device.

## Contract and provenance

The user's retail `stvoy.exe` and `stvoyHM.exe` 1.20 are the primary evidence.
The GPLv2 engine side of
[VoyagerSP-Android](https://github.com/imjustadudegamer/VoyagerSP-Android/tree/0d8942e86a8469859da086f590875bcb66b4f4df)
was also inspected; its licence was checked before use. The new byte reader,
loader, helpers and fixtures are independently written. No reference
implementation text, executable bytes or retail assets are incorporated.

| Contract | Observed retail behaviour | fnTech3 |

| Compressed bones | SP `0x462f70`: three unsigned 16-bit translations biased by 32768 and divided by 64, then nine matrix components biased by 32768 and divided by 32766 | Endian-independent byte reads; no alignment assumption or quaternion normalization |

## Bounded loading

`tr_mdr_format.h` validates the complete immutable input before `R_LoadMDR`
allocates renderer memory or registers a shader. It checks counts, signed
compressed-frame offsets, every nested record range, the expanded allocation
size, global bone indices, triangles, tags, reference lists and finite geometry.
The loader rebuilds offsets and disk-only identifiers in owned storage. Both
compressed and uncompressed files work, including unaligned input.

The installed corpus contains 79 vertices with NaN normals. Their weights and
positions are usable. fnTech3 replaces these normals with zero rather than
propagating NaNs into lighting. Likewise, it retains bounded light-grid sampling
at the high edge instead of reproducing retail's out-of-range read. These are
deliberate safety differences, not exact parity claims for malformed data.

## Numerical evidence

Native Windows x86 probes call the user's retail mathematical routines with
synthetic inputs and separately with the installed RDM5 files. The probes do
not start a game, emulate retail success, or reconstruct a game module.

- All 201 effective installed RDM5 models validate and load. All are compressed;
  199 contain three LODs and two contain one.
- All 2,081,643 compressed bones produce bit-identical decoded components.
- Skinning 41,865 surface poses and 1,973,385 vertices through retail and the
  production GLx skinning routine gives no index or UV differences. Maximum
  position error is `0.0000610351562` and maximum finite-normal error is
  `0.000000178813934`; x87/SSE evaluation is not bit-identical. Retail emits
  3,555 non-finite normal components in those poses; fnTech3 emits finite ones.
- Each retail mode matches all 480 synthetic LOD cases, including midpoint
  rounding, scale values above 20 and positive/negative biases.
- Ten retail light-grid vectors and ten complete entity-lighting results match
  the renderer calculations within the recorded floating-point tolerances;
  packed ambient colours match exactly. Seven diffuse-colour vectors match
  exactly, including half-integer values and saturation.

`mdr_format_tests` executes the production loader as C and C++, checks every
fixture truncation, byte mutations, invalid indices and offsets, allocation
guards, failed-load atomicity, frame selection and cross-profile LOD isolation.
`stvef_mdr_render_tests` executes each renderer's actual skinning, grid, entity
lighting and diffuse-colour functions. `rtcw_light_tests` additionally retains
the shared lighting checks for Q3, QL and RTCW.

The final Windows x86 MSVC build completes with all three renderers. The full
Meson suite passes 120 targets with two conditional skips; its Python target
passes 1,836 tests with 72 conditional skips. The six intermediate fixture
failures were missing helper declarations in an already-running test process;
the corrected fixture and the final full run pass.

## Windowed observations and limits

Retail and fnTech3 load the same retail-written `tutorial` save with isolated
writable directories under `E:\Games\fnTech3-tests`. The test uses 640 by 480
windows, disables mouse and joystick input, and obtains images solely through
the engines' `screenshot` commands. No operating-system capture or input
injection is used. Three GLx runs complete the saved-scene/model probe and produce byte-identical
fnTech3 captures. A second probe spawns and draws a
textured Tuvok NPC in the saved level in retail and three GLx runs. Its
transporter effect is allowed to settle before capture. The retail captures
reproduce the scene and character, but complete images are not byte-identical
to fnTech3; idle animation timing is not synchronized.

With the corrected defaults, matched texture settings and the same save, the
mean absolute difference from retail is below 0.75 per 8-bit colour channel
for both the background and character captures. This is a measurement of those
particular images, not a general visual tolerance or a substitute for the
native mathematical comparisons. The three GLx backgrounds are identical;
the NPC's idle poses can differ slightly between runs.

The [evidence manifest](2026-09-20-stvef-mdr.json) records the build,
retail identities, model identities, commands, renderer hashes and outcomes.
Local native probes and logs remain under `.tmp/stvef-gaps` in the isolated
`stvef-support` worktree; proprietary inputs and captures are not committed.

Retail probe setup exposed two separate limitations: its logging recurses with
`developer 1` and `logfile 2`, and its default 10 MB zone cannot load the
installed high-resolution texture pack. The successful probes disable developer
logging, set `com_FirstTime 1` to retain the chosen renderer settings, and use
128 MB of zone memory and 256 MB of hunk memory. A longer settling period is
needed before retail has a live player state available to save.

The corpus and runtime evidence establish this model/lighting slice on Windows
x86. Broader installation/platform coverage, exhaustive effect and shadow
comparisons, and audible movie parity remain separate open evidence gates.
