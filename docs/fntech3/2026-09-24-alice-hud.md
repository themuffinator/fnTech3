# Alice's HUD: the gauges and the two foldouts

## Scope

This record covers AliceHud, the in-game HUD of American McGee's Alice, as
the retail November 2000 `alice.exe` draws it, and what fnTech3 now serves of
it: the health and mana gauges (already served) and the weapon and powerup
foldouts (new), with the player-state and shared-block inputs that drive
them. It also records a wire-codec correction the weapon foldout exposed:
the player state's six masked arrays are signed shorts.

Addresses are into `alice.exe`, image base `0x00400000`, unless they start
`0x100` (`base/fgamex86.dll`). Every address below was read from the retail
binary with a disassembler (capstone) for this record. Observed facts and
inferences are kept apart; inferences say so. Nothing from Ritual's
F.A.K.K.2 SDK or from any reconstruction is incorporated as text.

## Observed: AliceHud and its children

AliceHud is built by `UI_Init` (`call 0x004110d0` at `0x00410bc9`); its
constructor (`0x00411080`) sets vtable `0x004ff584`, whose Draw
(`+0x0c`, `0x00411bf0`) is a bare `ret`. Everything the HUD shows is one of
eight children, each a `UIWidget3D` (constructor `0x004d22d0`, vtable
`0x0050096c`) or a subclass overriding only the destructor (`+0x04`) and Draw
(`+0x0c`):

| # | Model | Offset (640x480 from centre) | Scale | Class, Draw |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | `models/hud_health_back.tik` | -312, -25 | 2.45 | UIWidget3D, `0x004d2b10` |
| 2 | `models/hud_health_riser.tik` | -312, -25 | 2.45 | riser `0x004ff5d0`, `0x00411d50`, stat 0 |
| 3 | `models/hud_foldout_knife.tik` | -275, 182 | 6.0 | weapon foldout `0x004ff62c`, `0x004121c0` |
| 4 | `models/hud_health_bar.tik` | -312, 42 | 1.2 | UIWidget3D |
| 5 | `models/hud_itemfoldout.tik` | 275, 182 | 6.0 | item foldout `0x004ff67c`, `0x00412a00` |
| 6 | `models/hud_mana_back.tik` | 312, -25 | 2.45 | UIWidget3D |
| 7 | `models/hud_mana_riser.tik` | 312, -25 | 2.45 | riser, stat 8 |
| 8 | `models/hud_mana_bar.tik` | 312, 42 | 1.2 | UIWidget3D |

The offsets go through `0x004d2670`, the scale through `0x004d2990`, the
stat through `0x004d2970`; every child gets a 640 by 480 frame
(`0x004d2690`) and the animation `idle` (`0x004d27c0`). The model pushes are
at `0x0041124d`, `0x0041139b`, `0x00411508`, `0x00411656`, `0x004117a4`,
`0x004118f2`, `0x00411a40` and `0x00411bad`. Each child is appended to its
parent's list (`0x004bc06b`-`0x004bc077`), which is re-sorted
(`0x004bf7c0`) only when a child's layer bits (`+0x184 & 0x30`) differ from
its predecessor's.

Every Draw resets the angles to (0, 180, 0) (`0x004d2b3a`, `0x00412200`,
`0x00412a0f`) and renders through `0x004d29c0` with a 30-degree field of view
and 0.268 as the tangent of half of it; the foldouts, like the gauges, pass
no light (the three trailing arguments are zero).

**The riser's constructor** (`0x00411c00`) starts its target at 100
(`+0x238 = 0x64`, `0x00411c22`) and its shown value, ease start and timer at
zero. A first stat of 100 therefore shows a full gauge at once rather than
easing up from zero.

## Observed: the weapon foldout (Draw `0x004121c0`)

Its members, as its constructor (`0x00411ee0`) and AliceHud's init leave
them: `+0x238` a timer (0); `+0x23c` the table entry shown (0, the knife);
`+0x240` a string, the item's name, written and never read; `+0x244` a byte,
the new card is up (1); `+0x248` open (1); `+0x24c` the fold (0 idle, 1
unfolding, 2 folding shut); `+0x250` the dice count last shown (0);
`+0x21c` the frame (0, from `SetAnim`).

**Its input** is the player state's word at `0x00c30260`. The snapshot
player state starts at `0x00c30124` (the risers read stat *n* at `[n*4 +
0x00c301d4]`, and the block array is at +176), so this is `ps + 316`,
block 1 entry 3. The game module writes it (`0x1010d6f8`-`0x1010d747`):
-1, then, when the player holds an active weapon (`0x1013b070(0)`), what the
weapon's virtual `+0xcc` answers -- its item number, by inference, since the
probe below sees each such value resolve to the weapon's name through the
inventory and the item configstrings. `0x00407500` looks that
number up among block 2's 32 entries (`0x00c302d4`-`0x00c30354`) and, if
found, answers `CL_ConfigString(0x400 + n)` (`0x0042b640`, which drops with
`CL_ConfigString: bad index` outside 0 to 0x52f); otherwise null. Items are
configstrings `0x400` on, 64 of them, as the game's item-index import shows.

The name is matched case-blind (`0x0044fc10`, ASCII letters folded) against
a table of ten at `0x0050c668`, count at `0x0050c6b8`:

| Entry | Item | Card |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | `knife` | `models/hud_foldout_knife.tik` |
| 1 | `cards` | `models/hud_foldout_cards.tik` |
| 2 | `mallet` | `models/hud_foldout_mallet.tik` |
| 3 | `demondice` | `models/hud_foldout_dice1.tik` |
| 4 | `jackbomb` | `models/hud_foldout_jackbomb.tik` |
| 5 | `icewand` | `models/hud_foldout_icewand.tik` |
| 6 | `jacks` | `models/hud_foldout_jacks.tik` |
| 7 | `eyestaff` | `models/hud_foldout_eyestaff.tik` |
| 8 | `blunderbuss` | `models/hud_foldout_blunderbuss.tik` |
| 9 | `watch` | `models/hud_foldout_watchweapon.tik` |

No match makes the entry -1; a word of -1 skips the lookup. **A null name
leaves the item number itself as the entry** (`0x00412274` keeps the
register), which later indexes the table unchecked.

**One Draw**, in order:

1. Idle only (`+0x24c == 0`): a valid entry different from a valid shown
   one replaces the card at once, keeping the frame (`0x0041229c`-
   `0x004122cd`: the frame is saved around `SetModel` and `SetAnim`).
2. Idle only: while the entry is 3 and the shared block's dice count
   (`+0x38`) is non-zero, a count that differs from `+0x250` puts up
   `models/hud_foldout_dice` + the count (`0x00450180`, `"%d"`) + `.tik`
   (`0x0050ca94`, `0x0050c098`), without `SetAnim`; `+0x250` takes the count.
3. Idle only (`0x0041245f`-`0x004124c4`): with a card shown, losing the
   weapon while open starts a 200 ms fold shut (`+0x238 = 200`,
   `+0x24c = 2`). With none shown and a *named* weapon arriving, a shut card
   starts a 200 ms unfold and clears `+0x244`; an open one folds shut
   instead. The entry is then stored as the one shown.
4. Folding shut (`0x0041257e`-`0x004125d5`): while the timer is positive it
   loses the frame time (`0x00c239e4`) and the frame is `max(0, timer *
   0.005 * 50) + 50`, 100 down to 50; once it is not, the fold ends and the
   card is shut (`+0x248 = 0`).
5. Unfolding (`0x004125e2`-`0x00412633`): the same without the 50, 50 down
   to 0; at the end the card is open.
6. `0x00412641`: if the new card is not up and the frame is below 50, it
   goes up -- the dice card or the table's card for the *entry just read*
   -- keeping the frame.
7. The yaw becomes `180 + sin(0x00aa65e8 * 0.003003)` (`0x00412838`-
   `0x0041285d`); `0x00aa65e8` is copied at `0x0040e440` from `0x00c239e8`,
   the word after the frame time, which the subtitle box also reads as its
   clock. The frame is truncated and drawn unclamped.

## Observed: the item foldout (Draw `0x00412a00`)

Members (constructor `0x004128a0`): `+0x238` a timer, `+0x244` the state (0
hidden, 1 unfolding, 2 timing), `+0x248` the powerup shown (1 watch, 2 rage
box, 3 tea, 4 glass), `+0x24c` the time at pickup, not initialised; the frame
is set to 0 at the top of every Draw (`0x00412a1f`).

It reads the shared block (`0x0042c490`, `mov eax, 0x00c300a0`), and draws
nothing without one. In order of priority (`0x00412a34`-`0x00412bb0`):
`+0x2c` the rage box, `+0x30` the looking glass, `+0x28` the tea, `+0x34` the
watch. The first flag set puts its card up -- `models/hud_foldout_ragebox.tik`
(`0x0050c760`), `_glass` (`0x0050c730`), `_tea` (`0x0050c704`), `_watch`
(`0x0050c6e4`) -- with `SetAnim`, stores the truncated time at `+0x20` as the
pickup time, and starts a 200 ms unfold -- unless that powerup's card is
already up, in any state but hidden. With no flag set, an unfolding card turns to timing
with the timer at 200, a hidden one draws nothing, and a timing one carries
on.

- **Unfolding** (`0x00412c33`-`0x00412c86`): the frame is `max(0, timer *
  0.25)` while the timer is positive; then the state becomes timing with the
  timer at 1, and that frame draws at 0.
- **Timing** (`0x00412b30`-`0x00412b92`): a timer not above zero hides the
  card and clears the kind; otherwise, with `x` the time left over the time
  at pickup, the frame is `max(0, x * 35 + 15) + 50` (`0x004ff6cc`,
  `0x004ff6c8`), a full timer at 100 and an empty one at 65. When `x` reads
  zero -- or is NaN, which the x87 compare reports as equal -- the card hides
  at once. The timer is never counted down in this state.
- The frame is held to 0..100 (`0x00412c8c`-`0x00412cb8`), a NaN reading as
  below zero, and drawn without the wobble.

## Observed: the shared block's HUD members

The game module fetches the block through import 107
(`call [0x101e8ebc]`) and writes the HUD's members once a player update
(`0x101000f2`-`0x101001a1`), `ebp` being the player:

| Offset | Written as |
| --- | --- |
| `+0x28` | player `+0x214 & 1` |
| `+0x2c` | player `+0x214 & 2` |
| `+0x30` | player `+0x214 & 0x10` |
| `+0x34` | 1 when the float at `0x101fc3dc` exceeds the one at `0x101fc3cc`, else 0 |
| `+0x24` | player `+0x3b8` (not read by the HUD) |
| `+0x20` | player `+0x218` as a float when any of the first three is set; else `[0x101fc3dc] - [0x101fc3cc]` with the watch flag set; else 0 |
| `+0x38` | the `demondice` item's virtual `+0xb4` (`0x1013a700` finds it), or 0 without one |

`0x101fc3dc` is written as `[0x101fc3cc] + argument` by `0x100d12f0`
(called with 5.0 at `0x100af29a` and from `0x100f4960`) and cleared at
`0x100b9103`. InitGame also stores a module function at `+0x18`
(`0x100a36c5`).

## Observed: the wire carries the arrays as signed shorts

`MSG_ReadDeltaPlayerstate` (`0x004468e0`) reads each of the six arrays' set
entries through `MSG_ReadShort` (`call 0x00445900` at `0x00446aa9`), whose
sixteen bits are sign-extended (`movsx eax, ax` at `0x00445920`). fnTech3's
reader zero-extended them, so the -1 of an empty hand arrived as 65,535: the
first probe below shows `item 65535`, and a card that never folded.

## Observed: what AliceHud does not draw

- No ammunition, inventory list, pickup list or crosshair: AliceHud has only
  the eight children above. `ui_crosshair` and `ui_jumpreticle` are
  registered (`0x004105a0`, `0x004105b6`) into `0x00531050` and `0x005315f4`,
  which nothing in the engine reads; the crosshair belongs to the client
  game.
- A `ui_hud [1|0]` command handler exists at `0x0040fbe0` (it sets the
  cvar's integer without its string and shows or hides AliceHud) but no
  instruction or data word refers to it; it is never registered.
- A further UIWidget3D subclass (constructor `0x00412d00`, vtable
  `0x004ff6d0`, Draw `0x00412e40`: a lit model at 265, 205, scale 5) is never
  constructed.
- The `UIFakkLabel` stat and item links (`0x0041ef31`, `0x0041f042`) read
  the same player-state arrays for the `.urc` menus; they are menu widgets,
  not AliceHud.

## Observed: the installation's HUD assets

All 25 models the HUD can name are in `pak0.pk3`, among them
`hud_foldout_dice.tik`, `dice1` to `dice3` and `watchweapon`. Every foldout
TIKI animates `idle` with `models/ui/hud/foldout/folding.tan` or
`.../itemfoldout/folding.tan`; both are `TAN ` version 2 with 101 frames.
`models/w_demondice.tik` sets `maxamount 3` and caches `dice1` to `dice3`.

## Inferences

- Frame 0 is the card unfolded, 50 folded shut and 100 unfolded again: the
  weapon card rests at 0 when open and at 50 when shut, a powerup's card
  goes from 0 to 100 at the start of its timing with no visible jump, and
  the probe frames below show the card open at both ends and gone at 50.
- The dice count at `+0x38` is the number of dice held: the value selects
  among `dice1` to `dice3`, and the weapon's `maxamount` is 3. The virtual
  itself is not named.
- The powerup time is in seconds: the game wrote 42 for the rage box, 45 for
  the glass and 26 for the tea as whole numbers (the glass read 45 and then
  44 in `.tmp/alice-hud/powerups1`), and the watch arm reads a float
  difference of level times.
- `0x101fc3cc` is the level time and `0x101fc3dc` the end of the level's
  deadtime, the watch's slowdown: every system reads the first, and the
  second is only ever set to the first plus a duration. The probe saw the
  watch flag rise with the rage box pickup and show the watch's card for
  its last five seconds after the tea ran out.

## In fnTech3

- `code/compat/alice_hud.hpp` (new): the widget table, the riser, the
  weapon table and its lookup, both foldouts' state machines, and the shared
  block's HUD members, as pure functions.
- `code/client/cl_alice_hud.cpp`: the eight children drawn in creation order
  through one render path; the weapon foldout's inputs from
  `cl.snap.ps.alice.block[1][3]`, `block[2]` and the item configstrings; the
  item foldout's from `Alice_SharedBlock()`. Model handles are cached by
  name and dropped when the renderer restarts; the widgets' state is kept,
  as retail builds them once. Developer builds print each weapon-foldout
  input change and each item-foldout state or flag change (`Com_DPrintf`).
- `code/compat/alice_message_codec.hpp`: `ReadBlock` sign-extends each
  sixteen-bit value.

### Where fnTech3 differs, and why

- A table entry outside 0..9 -- a nameless item number, or -1 reaching the
  step-6 swap -- puts no card up; retail reads past its table
  (`0x0050c664` holds `0x00000a73`, which is not a pointer).
- An inventory item whose configstring index would pass 1,327 has no name,
  where retail drops the client.
- The item's name at `+0x240` is not kept; nothing reads it.
- Float arithmetic is double precision where retail's is the x87's 80 bits;
  a zero pickup time divides to the same infinity or NaN explicitly.
- The riser's target now starts at 100, as retail's constructor sets it; the
  previous code started it at -99,999, which eased every gauge up from empty
  on the first frame.
- The developer prints are fnTech3's own.

## Results

A developer client, `meson/build/win32-alice-hud` configured with
`-Dalice-development=true`, at `2d35919d0f6ee62f869f34186a4b11d3e189742f`
plus the shared tree's uncommitted Alice work and this change. The final
build is `fntech3.exe`
`234ce355027bc8fd99ed577e63996abdaef8f87361ce2dec0a9073ce33e18fb4`; the
runs `fold2`, `burst1` and `powerups2` used
`ac805fbc3fa7c7e7770031b7da9f3b1cc194987ffae745bcd4ce8d21e1c7c108`, which
differs from it only by an unused include and a comment; both with
`fnql_glx_x86.dll`
`03baeb618416855751b6a1245e65efd5195f9958f658448c79a0f54a43ff9eb3`. Every
run was windowed at 800 by 600 on a private desktop with no input injected,
with a home path under `.tmp/alice-hud/<run>/home`:

```
fntech3.exe +set com_gameProfile alice +set fs_basepath E:\Games\Alice
  +set fs_homepath <run>\home +set fs_game "" +set fs_cdpath ""
  +set r_fullscreen 0 +set r_mode -1 +set r_customWidth 800
  +set r_customHeight 600 +set in_mouse 0 +set in_joystick 0 +set in_nograb 1
  +set s_initsound 0 +set com_steamIntegration 0 +set fs_skipWorkshop 1
  +set cl_webuiEnable 0 +set cl_renderer glx +set logfile 4
  +set net_ip 127.0.0.1 +set net_port 0 +set developer 1
  +exec alice-probe.cfg +devmap garden1
```

`alice-probe.cfg` sets `activeAction` to exec a step file once the client is
active: `wait 12000`, `skipcinematic` (the game module's own command, which
ends the level's opening scene), then the console commands below with
`wait` in milliseconds and the engine's `screenshotPNG`. The installation's
file inventory was identical before and after every run.

- **Before the codec correction** (`.tmp/alice-hud/powerups1`, `fntech3.exe`
  `4da1e86ec717bcb4fa9aa02196da54405189d9d35f503cbe21ad47897f5a96f7`): the
  developer print shows the empty hand as `item 65535 "" -> entry 65535`, so
  the knife card the widget is built with stayed up with no weapon in hand
  (`.tmp/alice-hud/weapons1/hud-a02-knife.png`, an earlier build without
  the input print). The same runs show the mallet's and the two dice's cards
  (`weapons1/hud-a03-mallet.png`, `hud-a05-dice.png`).
- **The weapon foldout** (`.tmp/alice-hud/fold2`): with no weapon the card
  folds shut at once (`item -1 -> entry -1, fold 2, frame 96`, then `fold 0,
  frame 50`) and `hud-e01-unarmed.png` shows none. `give w_mallet.tik; use
  mallet` unfolds the mallet's card (`fold 1, frame 46`, then `fold 0, frame

  82.25` without restarting it, `fold 0, frame 50`, then the swap at `fold 1,
  frame 48.25`).
- **The powerup foldout** (`.tmp/alice-hud/powerups2`): `give w_ragebox.tik`
  unfolds the rage box's card at the lower right (`state 1, powerup 2 (…,
  rage box 2, …, watch 1), time 42 of 42`; `hud-g01` to `hud-g04`), still up
  eleven seconds on (`hud-g05-rage-11s.png`). `give w_lookingglass.tik`
  replaces the flags and its card (`powerup 4`, time 45; `hud-g06`);
  `give w_grasshopperteaitem.tik` puts up the tea's (`powerup 3`, time 26;
  `hud-g07`). When the tea ran out the watch's card came up for the deadtime
  left (`powerup 1, time 5.3 of 5`) and hid when its flag cleared
  (`state 0 … watch 0`); `hud-g08-later.png` shows no card.
- **The final build** (`.tmp/alice-hud/final`): unarmed, the card folds shut
  (`hud-final-01-unarmed.png`); `use mallet` unfolds the mallet's card
  (`hud-final-02-mallet.png`); `use demondice` with two dice swaps to
  `hud_foldout_dice2.tik` (`hud-final-03-dice2.png`); `give w_ragebox.tik`
  unfolds the rage box's card beside it (`hud-final-04-rage.png`, both
  foldouts up at once).
- The first probe (`.tmp/alice-hud/baseline`, before this change) shows the
  opening scene's letterbox and subtitle 15 s after `devmap garden1`; the

## Tests

  the riser's constructor target, its ease, the timer it leaves unwritten and
  its clamps; the fold curve; the weapon table, case-blind lookup, dice model
  names and the unnamed-item rule; the weapon foldout's swap, fold shut,

  shared block's offsets; the item foldout's priority, unfold, timing frames,

  signed shorts, and a -1 and a -40 cross as themselves.
- All twenty `fntech3_alice_*` C++ tests pass in `win32-alice-hud`, as do
  `tests/alice_demo_tests.py`, `alice_cinematic_tests.py`,

  `alice_shader_parse_tests.py`, and the `meson.build` readers
  `ci_regression_workflow_tests.py`, `x86_build_constraints_tests.py` and
  `platform_port_safety_tests.py`.

## Still open

- No retail screenshot has been set beside these: retail `alice.exe` has not
  been run here, so placement and occlusion are checked against the
  disassembly only.
- The watch's own use (`deadtimewatch` on its fire animation) was not
  exercised, since that needs the fire button; the watch card was seen only
  through the deadtime the rage box started.
- Draw order is the list's append order, taken as unchanged by the re-sort
  because the eight children are built alike; their layer bits were not read.
- Only the GL renderer was run.
